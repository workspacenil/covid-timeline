// 01 Know the virus

Anatomy of a coronavirus

SARS-CoV-2 is the virus that causes COVID-19. It is called a coronavirus because the spikes on its surface look like a crown. Tap a part.

S

Spike protein. Lets the virus attach to the receptors on our cells. The spikes are the target of most vaccines.

ENV

Envelope. A fatty outer layer that wraps the virus. Soap breaks it apart, which is why hand washing works.

RNA

Genome. A single strand of RNA with the instructions to make new viruses inside an infected cell.

On March 11, the WHO declares COVID-19 a pandemic. Lockdowns and movement restrictions are applied in many countries.

VACUNES · VACCINES
5 MAIG 2023 · MAY 5, 2023

Fi de l'emergència · The emergency ends

CA

L'OMS anuncia el final de l'emergència de salut pública internacional: feia més d'un any que la pandèmia baixava, amb molta immunitat per vacunes i contagis, menys morts i hospitals menys saturats. Però avisa que el virus no ha desaparegut.

EN

The WHO announces the end of the international public health emergency: the pandemic had been declining for over a year, with high immunity from vaccines and infections, fewer deaths and less pressure on hospitals. But it warns that the virus has not disappeared.

FI DE L'EMERGÈNCIA · EMERGENCY LIFTED
AVUI · TODAY

La COVID-19 avui · COVID-19 today

CA

El virus continua circulant i encara causa morts, encara que moltes menys que durant la pandèmia. L'OMS encara registra més de mil morts notificades cada mes, sobretot de gent gran. Les vacunes protegeixen de la malaltia greu.

EN

The virus is still circulating and still causes deaths, although far fewer than during the pandemic. The WHO still records over a thousand reported deaths every month, mostly of older people. Vaccines protect against severe disease.
